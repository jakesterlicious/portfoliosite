All work

Pens.com Customizer

Transforming the personalization experience on a major e-commerce site.

Role
Lead Product Designer
Timeline
3 months (Q2 2024)
Tools
Figma, Google Analytics, Hotjar, UserTesting

The challenge

The legacy customizer lost people at the customization step, where analytics showed a 42% drop-off. Long scrolling, a limited preview and a fragmented mobile experience made it hard to see what you were building. The goal was a customization flow that works the same way on every screen.

Research and discovery

Three sources shaped the direction.

Analytics

42% drop-off rate at the customization step.

User feedback

15 user interviews revealed confusion around pricing.

Competitive analysis

Reviewed 8 competitors for best-in-class customization.

User testing results

Unmoderated user tests on the customizer showed that the lack of flexibility was the number one issue across customizer types.

User testing findings (Figma Slides)

Defining the strategy

Research synthesis pointed to three core problems.

  • The long scrolling of the page made it unintuitive to design the product
  • Limited preview capabilities meant users could not see the result as they worked
  • Fragmented mobile experience led to high abandonment

Ideation and wireframing

Multiple wireframe versions led from a structural sketch to a mid-fidelity mobile design.

Final design

The redesigned customizer is a single interface that works across devices.

Unified customizer
One coherent interface.
Persistent CTA
The next step stays in reach.
Real-time preview
Instant visual feedback as users make selections.
Smooth animations
Transitions that keep the flow fluid.
App-like toolbar
Logo, Text, Layout and Product tools in one place.

Desktop screenshot

Results and impact

The redesigned customizer launched in June 2024.

↑ 9%

Conversion rate increase

↓ 42%

Support calls about customization

↑ 24%

Mobile customization completion

Key learnings

Mobile-first is essential

With 60% of traffic coming from mobile devices, optimizing for mobile came first.

Stakeholder input is crucial

Users need to see pricing and preview their customization.

Test early and often

Usability testing at the wireframe stage saved significant effort.

Data drives decisions

Combining quantitative analytics with qualitative research.
